<h3 align="center">Small tools, sharp edges, no drama.</h3>

I build interfaces in TypeScript and React, usually Next.js or Vite, and I care a lot about the parts people don't notice until they're wrong — focus rings, layout shift, the half-second before anything paints. Design systems are where that obsession pays off, since one good decision gets inherited by everyone downstream. I'm still not sure why some pages feel quick before the numbers agree, but I keep measuring to find out.
